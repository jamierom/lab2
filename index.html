Welcome, I am Jamie Romano!

I am a third-year CCIT student at UofT interested in all things tech, design and web development. Here is more to my profile showcase with CCT260, my first ever web-development class I took in second-year.

Project One: A Website Redesign

Me and a few of my classmates worked on a website re-design for clothing site, Comme des Garcons. We looked into pain-points of the site (like extreme visuals, lack of identification) to construct a new design for the web-page with menu bars and product categories. This was used with Figma.

Project Two: A Recipe for Halo Halo

One of my first ever solo web projects was working on a recipe page for famous Filipino dessert, Halo Halo. Using simple CSS and HTML, I used lists (for ingredients) and numbered lists (for instructions) with vibrant colors to make a colorful and neat page for viewers to follow.

Project Three: Fanpage for Chainsaw Man

One of my favorite solo web projects was for creating a fanpage to promote the latest Chainsaw Man movie, the Reze Arc. This was one of my favorite anime and movies to watch so it was fun learning about the characters (for descriptions) and importing and imbedding gifs, videos and images to a page.
