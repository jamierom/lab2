Welcome, I am Jamie!

I am a third-year CCIT student at UofT interested in all things tech, design and web development. Here are some of the projects I've done in CCT260, my first web development class that I took in second-year.

Project One: A Website Redesign

A few of my classmates and I worked on a website redesign for the clothing site Comme des Garcons. We looked into pain points of the site (like extreme visuals, lack of identification) to construct a new design for the webpage with menu bars and product categories. This was made with Figma.

Project Two: A Recipe for Halo Halo

One of my first solo web projects was working on a recipe page for the famous Filipino dessert, Halo Halo. Using simple CSS and HTML, I used lists (for ingredients) and numbered lists (for instructions) with vibrant colors to make a colorful and neat page for viewers to follow.

Project Three: Fanpage for Chainsaw Man

One of my favorite solo web projects was creating a fanpage to promote the latest Chainsaw Man movie, the Reze Arc. This was one of my favorite anime and movies to watch, so it was fun learning about the characters and imbedding gifs, videos, and images into a page.
